A problem solver in software & hardware.

I'm a full stack software engineer based in Nairobi, with a heavy focus on backend development and system design. I hold a BSc. in Control and Instrumentation from JKUAT and I'm a certified software engineer through the ALX programme.

I focus on the solution and whether it will last. My work spans web applications, distributed systems, IoT and robotics, with a lot of time spent on Linux, multi-protocol communication platforms and backend services in Go, Node.js and Python.

I want to keep learning as much as I can and put it to work on real problems.
