01 / about

A problem solver in software & hardware.

I'm a self-taught engineer with a broad range of experience, which lets me take on different kinds of problems in different settings. I hold a BSc. in Control and Instrumentation from JKUAT and I'm a certified software engineer through the ALX programme.

As a software engineer I focus on the solution and whether it will last. My work spans web applications, distributed systems, IoT and robotics. I've spent a lot of time on Linux systems, multi-protocol communication platforms and backend services in Go, Node.js and Python.

I want to keep learning as much as I can and put it to work on real problems.
